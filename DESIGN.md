## 1. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY & FOUNDATION

### 1.1 The Beau Foundation
The visual language of the STI 2026 digital yearbook is built on the **Beau** style system from Refero Styles:
- **Core Concept:** Ink on warm parchment — a confident, monochromatic editorial experience with a single theatrical gradient spotlight.
- **Atmosphere:** An editorial monograph, architectural yearbook, or high-end photography publication. Hushed, authoritative, timeless, and deeply human.
- **Reference URL:** [Refero Styles — Beau](https://styles.refero.design/style/c73ba3d8-42fe-4d53-bec1-b6643949c582)

### 1.2 What is Adopted from Beau
| Aspect | Specification | Role in STI 2026 |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Palette** | Paper White (`#ffffff`), Warm Parchment (`#f6f4f1`), Ink Black (`#000000`), Soft Graphite (`#666666`), Mid Ash (`#999999`), Pale Mist (`#b3b3b3`) | Monochromatic editorial dignity; warm paper canvas stops the page from feeling clinical or stark. |
| **Accent** | Broadcast Gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgb(255, 131, 8), rgb(255, 80, 67) 50%, rgb(57, 43, 213))`) | The lone theatrical spotlight; rationed with ceremony for featured project frames, category pills, and at most one section band. |
| **Typography** | Geist (`--font-geist`) with weights 400 and 500 only; stylistic sets `ss01`, `ss03`, `ss04` enabled | Tight editorial tracking, modernist geometric letterforms, single authoritative voice across all text. |
| **Geometry** | 6px radius for cards and images; 200px (full pill) for buttons and badges | Strict contrast between structural rectilinear boxes (6px) and tactile pill buttons (200px). |
| **Elevation** | Subtle 2px atmospheric haze (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06) 0px 2px 6px`) | Matte, tactile surfaces. Absolutely no deep, floating, multi-layer drop shadows. |
| **Rhythm** | 72px section gap, 24px card padding, 1200px centered max-width | Unhurried, spacious density where each section breathes independently. |

### 1.3 What is NOT Taken from Beau (Explicit Rejections)
Beau in its original Refero manifestation is a B2B SaaS product presentation. For STI 2026, the following elements of Beau are **explicitly rejected and forbidden**:
- ❌ **No SaaS UI Mockups:** No fake document upload cards, no scanning progress meters, no form verification checklists, no "Analyzing..." widgets.
- ❌ **No SaaS Marketing CTAs:** No "Request Demo", "Start Free Trial", or pricing calculators.
- ❌ **No Software Frames as Sole Content:** In original Beau, UI screenshots dominate; in STI 2026, **authentic student photography and actual student projects** are the heroes.

## 2. ANTI-AI-SLOP RULES

To ensure the site looks human-crafted, prestigious, and enduring rather than generated by automated AI templates, the following practices are **strictly forbidden**:

1. **NO Generic SaaS Aesthetics:** Do not turn this digital yearbook into a corporate dashboard, developer tooling landing page, or startup marketing site.
2. **NO Excessive Gradients:** The Broadcast Gradient is permitted only in designated spotlight moments (feature pills, featured project frames, and maximum one section wash). Never apply gradients to text, general backgrounds, card borders across the board, or icons.
3. **NO Cyber / Hacker / Neon Aesthetics:** Strictly no terminal scan lines, matrix rain, glowing cyan/magenta borders, dark cyber themes, or sci-fi decorations. STI is a rigorous academic discipline, not a futuristic movie prop.
4. **NO Excessive Glassmorphism:** No translucent panels with heavy `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and glowing frosted borders. Surfaces are flat, warm, and matte paper.
5. **NO Random 3D Renders or Splines:** Strictly no floating 3D geometric shapes, iridescent spheres, isometric tech blobs, or stylized 3D human figures.
6. **NO Ambient Particles or Canvases:** No floating particles, constellations, mouse-chasing dots, or animated generative noise overlays.
7. **NO Decorative Empty Cards:** Every card must hold real student or project information. Never generate filler cards with stock icons just to fill a grid column.
8. **NO Meaningless Animations:** Never animate an element just because you can. Every motion must have a narrative purpose (e.g. revealing content as the user reads).

## 3. DESIGN TOKENS

### 3.1 Colors

| Name | Hex / Value | Token | Purpose & Application |
|------|-------------|-------|-----------------------|
| **Paper White** | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper-white` | Primary canvas for high-contrast sections, student card surfaces, lightbox backgrounds. |
| **Warm Parchment** | `#f6f4f1` | `--color-warm-parchment` | Alternate section background — provides cream warmth, visual pacing, and separates content without hairline dividers. |
| **Ink Black** | `#000000` | `--color-ink-black` | Primary headlines, body text, dark feature card fills, filled pill button background. The structural ink of the site. |
| **Soft Graphite** | `#666666` | `--color-soft-graphite` | Secondary text, team member lists, student nicknames, timeline dates, subtle borders. |
| **Mid Ash** | `#999999` | `--color-mid-ash` | Tertiary metadata, captions, image credits, inactive pagination dots. |
| **Pale Mist** | `#b3b3b3` | `--color-pale-mist` | Hairline dividers, subtle card borders, disabled button surfaces. |
| **Broadcast Gradient** | `linear-gradient(135deg, rgb(255, 131, 8), rgb(255, 80, 67) 50%, rgb(57, 43, 213))` | `--color-broadcast-gradient` | Signature spotlight accent: feature badges, project highlight rings, single section band wash. |

### 3.2 Typography

- **Primary Typeface:** `Geist` (`--font-geist`)
- **Fallback Typeface:** `Inter`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`
- **Permitted Weights:** `400` (Regular) and `500` (Medium) ONLY. Weights `600`, `700`, `800`, or `900` are **strictly forbidden** to protect the restrained editorial voice.
- **OpenType Feature Settings:** `"ss01" on, "ss03" on, "ss04" on` (activates alternate single-storey 'a', geometric 'g', and modernist '$').

#### Type Scale Table

| Level | Size | Line Height | Tracking (Letter Spacing) | Weight | Token | Application |
|-------|------|-------------|---------------------------|--------|-------|-------------|
| **display** | 56px | 1.10 | -1.12px (-0.02em) | 500 | `--text-display` | Hero headline, major celebratory yearmarks. |
| **heading-lg** | 40px | 1.15 | -0.80px (-0.02em) | 500 | `--text-heading-lg` | Primary section headlines (Students, Projects, Timeline). |
| **heading** | 33px | 1.15 | -0.66px (-0.02em) | 500 | `--text-heading` | Subsection headlines, student name on profile page. |
| **heading-sm** | 28px | 1.20 | -0.56px (-0.02em) | 500 | `--text-heading-sm` | Project title on detail page, event headings. |
| **subheading** | 20px | 1.30 | -0.20px (-0.01em) | 500 | `--text-subheading` | Student name in directory card, project card title. |
| **body** | 17px | 1.40 | -0.17px (-0.01em) | 400 | `--text-body` | Standard editorial reading copy, student bios, project descriptions. |
| **caption** | 14px | 1.40 | -0.14px (-0.01em) | 400 / 500 | `--text-caption` | Metadata, button text (500), badge labels (500), nicknames (400), image credits. |

### 3.3 Spacing Scale
- **Base Grid Unit:** 6px
- **Values:**
  - `--spacing-6`: 6px
  - `--spacing-12`: 12px
  - `--spacing-18`: 18px
  - `--spacing-24`: 24px (standard card padding & element gap)
  - `--spacing-30`: 30px
  - `--spacing-36`: 36px
  - `--spacing-48`: 48px
  - `--spacing-72`: 72px (standard section gap)
  - `--spacing-96`: 96px (hero top/bottom padding)

### 3.4 Geometry & Elevation
- **Card & Image Border Radius:** `6px` (`--radius-cards`, `--radius-images`) — subtle, restrained, never overly rounded or bubbly.
- **Button & Badge Border Radius:** `200px` (`--radius-buttons`, `--radius-badges`) — full pill shape.
- **Shadow:** `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06) 0px 2px 6px 0px` (`--shadow-sm`) — single atmospheric haze. No other drop shadow permitted.

## 4. STI 2026 COMPONENT SPECIFICATIONS

### 4.1 Pill Button (Primary & Ghost)
- **Filled Variant (Primary):**
  - Background: `#000000` (Ink Black)
  - Text: `#ffffff` (Paper White)
  - Typography: Geist 500, 14px, tracking -0.14px
  - Padding: 12px vertical, 18px horizontal
  - Radius: 200px
  - Hover: Background `#1a1a1a`, `translateY(-1px)` (desktop only)
  - Active: `scale(0.98)`
- **Ghost Variant (Secondary):**
  - Background: transparent; no border
  - Text: `#000000`
  - Typography: Geist 400, 14px
  - Hover: Text underline, opacity 0.75

### 4.2 Feature Badge / Pill
- **Default Variant:** Warm parchment background (`#f6f4f1`), Ink Black text (`#000000`), Geist 500 14px, 6px 12px padding, 200px radius.
- **Spotlight Variant:** Broadcast Gradient background, white text (`#ffffff`), Geist 500 14px, 6px 14px padding, 200px radius. Used for key achievements and featured project tags.

### 4.3 Student Directory Card (`StudentCard`)
- **Container:** Paper white surface (`#ffffff`), 6px radius, hairline border (`1px solid #e5e5e5`), 24px padding (or full-bleed image with 16px text block below).
- **Image:** 3:4 portrait ratio (or 1:1 square), `border-radius: 6px`, `object-fit: cover`.
- **Typography:**
  - Name: Geist 500, 20px (`--text-subheading`), `#000000`.
  - Nickname: Geist 400, 14px (`--text-caption`), `#666666`.
  - Interests: 1–3 default badges below.
- **Hover:** `translateY(-4px)` with shadow transition to `rgba(0,0,0,0.10) 0px 8px 24px 0px` (desktop only).
- **Fallback (No Photo):** Stylized warm parchment surface with monochrome student initials (e.g., "BS") rendered in Geist 500 33px `#000000`.

### 4.4 Project Card (`ProjectCard`)
- **Container:** 6px radius, `#ffffff` surface, optional atmospheric shadow.
- **Cover Image:** 16:9 aspect ratio, 6px radius, `object-fit: cover`.
- **Featured Accent:** Optional 3px outer ring filled with Broadcast Gradient wrapping the cover image (`GradientFrame`).
- **Typography:**
  - Title: Geist 500, 28px (`--text-heading-sm`), `#000000`.
  - Team: Geist 400, 14px, `#666666`.
  - Technology: Badges with tech names.
- **Hover:** `translateY(-4px)`, image zooms 1.03× (desktop only).

### 4.5 Campus Life & Memory Card (`MemoryCard`)
- **Container:** Borderless card on Warm Parchment or Paper White canvas.
- **Image:** 4:3 or 3:2 landscape photograph, 6px radius.
- **Caption:** Geist 400, 14px `#666666` with date in Mid Ash (`#999999`).
- **Interaction:** Hover scales image 1.04×; click opens full-screen accessible lightbox.

### 4.6 Timeline Component (`TimelineView`)
- **Track Line:** 2px solid `#e5e5e5` running vertically.
- **Node:** 12px circular indicator (black fill with 3px white halo).
- **Card:** Warm parchment or white card, 6px radius, containing date (Geist 500 14px), milestone title (Geist 500 20px), and description (Geist 400 17px).
- **Layout:** Alternating left/right on desktop (≥ 768px); single-column left-aligned on mobile (< 768px).

### 4.7 Navigation Bar (`SiteHeader`)
- **Bar:** Sticky top bar, `#ffffff` background with subtle border-bottom (`1px solid #f0f0f0`), height 72px.
- **Left:** STI 2026 wordmark in Geist 500 17px.
- **Center:** Nav links in Geist 400 14px (`#000000`).
- **Right:** Single filled pill button CTA (desktop).
- **Mobile (< 1024px):** Replaced by minimal hamburger icon linking to full-screen `NavDrawer`.

### 4.8 Section Heading Stack
- **Structure:**
  - Kicker / Category: Geist 500, 14px, `#666666`, uppercase with tracking +0.05em (optional).
  - Headline: Geist 500, 33–56px, `#000000`, tracking -0.66px to -1.12px.
  - Subhead: Geist 400, 17–20px, `#666666`, line-height 1.40.
- **Pacing:** Centered or left-aligned with 24px bottom gap before content grid.

## 5. PHOTOGRAPHY & IMAGERY GUIDELINES

1. **Authenticity Over Staging:** Real moments from university life — hackathons, labs, campus lawns, classroom debates. Avoid generic corporate stock photos.
2. **Editorial Grading:** Naturalistic warmth, rich blacks, neutral highlights. Avoid hypersaturated filters, vintage grain filters, or cinematic color washes.
3. **Student Portraits:**
   - Orientation: Vertical portrait (3:4) preferred; square (1:1) acceptable.
   - Framing: Head-and-shoulders or torso-up with clean, uncluttered background.
   - Consistency: Clean lighting, neutral or campus environmental background.
4. **Project Visuals:** High-resolution software screenshots, system architecture diagrams, or UI mockups set within 6px rounded frames.
